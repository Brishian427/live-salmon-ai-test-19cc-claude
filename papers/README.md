# Downloaded Papers

## Core Papers (Dead Salmon / Statistical Testing)

1. **Neural Correlates of Interspecies Perspective Taking in the Post-Mortem Atlantic Salmon** (bennett_2009_dead_salmon_fmri.pdf)
   - Authors: Bennett, Baird, Miller, Wolford
   - Year: 2009
   - Why relevant: The original "dead salmon" study — foundational inspiration for our research

2. **The Dead Salmons of AI Interpretability** (meloux_2025_dead_salmons_ai_interpretability.pdf)
   - Authors: Méloux, Dirupo, Portet, Peyrard
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: 2512.18792
   - Why relevant: Extends dead salmon concept to AI — shows interpretability methods produce plausible explanations for random networks

3. **Cluster Failure: Why fMRI Inferences Have Inflated False-Positive Rates** (eklund_2016_cluster_failure_fmri.pdf)
   - Authors: Eklund, Nichols, Knutsson
   - Year: 2016
   - arXiv: 1511.01863
   - Why relevant: Large-scale demonstration of systematic false positives in standard analytical pipelines

4. **Large-Scale Simultaneous Inference with Hypothesis Testing** (emmert_2019_multiple_testing.pdf)
   - Authors: Emmert-Streib, Dehmer
   - Year: 2019
   - Why relevant: Review of multiple testing procedures (FWER, FDR) — the statistical foundation for evaluating AI scientist rigor

## Evaluation and Critique

9. **The More You Automate, the Less You See: Hidden Pitfalls** (fang_2025_more_automate_less_see.pdf)
   - Authors: Luo, Kasirzadeh, Shah (CMU)
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: 2509.08713
   - Why relevant: Identifies four failure modes (benchmark selection, data leakage, metric misuse, post-hoc bias) — directly informs our evaluation framework

10. **Evaluating Sakana's AI Scientist** (fournier_2025_evaluating_ai_scientist.pdf)
    - Authors: Beel, Kan, Baumgart
    - Year: 2025
    - arXiv: 2502.14297
    - Why relevant: Independent evaluation showing 42% experiment failure rate, hallucinated results

11. **AI Scientists Fail Without Strong Implementation Capability** (huang_2025_ai_scientists_fail.pdf)
    - Authors: Zhu, Xie, Weng et al. (Westlake University)
    - Year: 2025
    - arXiv: 2506.01372
    - Why relevant: Position paper on implementation gap — AI idea generation exceeds execution capability
